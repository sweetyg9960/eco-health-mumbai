📖 1. Introduction

Introduction to the study of environmental and public health data in Mumbai

1.1 Background of the Study

Environmental conditions have a direct relationship with public health. Air pollution, particulate matter and changing environmental conditions can affect the quality of life of people. This project focuses on understanding environmental and public health data through data visualization.

1.2 Introduction to the Selected Topic

The selected topic is data visualization of public health and environmental data in Mumbai. The project presents information about air quality and PM2.5 levels using charts, tables and simple visual representations.

1.3 Problem Statement

Environmental data is often available in large amounts and may be difficult to understand in raw form. The project aims to present environmental and health-related information in a simple and understandable visual format.

1.4 Need for the Study

There is a need to understand environmental conditions and their possible impact on public health. Data visualization can help people identify changes and patterns in air quality data more easily.

1.5 Significance of the Study

The study helps in creating awareness about environmental conditions and public health. It also demonstrates how data visualization can make complex environmental information easier to understand.

1.6 Scope of the Study

The study focuses on Mumbai and includes environmental information related mainly to air quality and PM2.5 levels. The project can be further extended by adding more locations, health indicators and real-time environmental data.

1.7 Objectives of the Study

  • To study environmental and air quality data of Mumbai.
  • To understand the relationship between environment and public health.
  • To present environmental data using charts and tables.
  • To make environmental information simple and easy to understand.
  • To create awareness about environmental conditions through data visualization.

📚 2. Literature Review

Review of existing information related to environment and public health

2.1 Environmental Studies

Previous studies show that air pollution and environmental conditions can have a significant impact on public health. Air quality indicators such as AQI and PM2.5 are commonly used to understand pollution levels.

2.2 Air Pollution and Health

Research indicates that higher levels of air pollution may affect respiratory health and overall quality of life. Monitoring air quality helps in understanding these environmental health risks.

2.3 Data Visualization in Environmental Studies

Charts, graphs and tables make environmental data easier to understand. Data visualization helps identify changes and patterns in air quality and supports better public awareness.

2.4 Research Gap

There is a need for simple and understandable presentation of environmental and public health data. This project focuses on presenting Mumbai's environmental information through clear visualizations.

3. Research Methodology

Method used for collecting, organizing and analyzing environmental data

3.1 Research Design

This project follows a descriptive research design to study environmental and public health-related data of Mumbai. The study focuses on presenting information about air quality, PM2.5 levels and other environmental indicators through simple charts, tables and visual representations. The collected information is organized and analyzed to identify important patterns and trends.

3.2 Study Area

The study area selected for this project is Mumbai, Maharashtra. Mumbai is considered for the study because environmental conditions such as air pollution and air quality can have an important relationship with public health. The project focuses on understanding and visualizing environmental data related to Mumbai.

3.3 Data Collection Methods

Data for this project is collected from relevant environmental and public health information sources. The collected information includes air quality and PM2.5-related data. The data is organized in a suitable format and presented through tables and charts for easier understanding and analysis.

3.4 Primary Data

Primary data refers to information collected directly from respondents or through field-based observations. For this project, primary data can include information collected through questionnaires, surveys or direct observations related to environmental awareness and public health.

3.5 Secondary Data

Secondary data refers to information collected from already available sources. For this project, secondary data includes environmental and public health information obtained from reliable reports, government sources and publicly available environmental data.

3.6 Tools Used for Data Analysis

The collected data is organized and analyzed using tables, charts and graphical representations. HTML, CSS and JavaScript are used to present the environmental data on the website, while charts are used to make comparisons and trends easier to understand.

📊 Data Analysis and Interpretation

Analysis and graphical representation of environmental data

5.1 Introduction

The collected environmental data is analyzed to understand changes in air quality and PM2.5 levels. Charts and graphs are used to present the data in a simple and understandable manner.

5.2 Data Profile

The project uses monthly environmental data for Mumbai. The main indicators considered are Air Quality Index (AQI) and PM2.5 levels.

5.3 Analysis of Data

The monthly AQI and PM2.5 values are compared to identify changes in air quality across different months.

5.4 Month-wise Data Analysis

  • AQI values are compared from January to June.
  • PM2.5 levels are compared from January to June.
  • The graphs show monthly changes in the selected indicators.

5.5 Graphical Representation of Data

Bar charts and line charts are used to visually represent AQI and PM2.5 data. Graphical representation makes comparison and trends easier to understand.

5.6 Interpretation of Results

The visualization shows that AQI and PM2.5 values vary across the selected months. Such variations help in understanding changes in environmental conditions.

5.7 Major Observations

  • AQI shows variation across the selected months.
  • PM2.5 levels also change from month to month.
  • Data visualization makes environmental trends easier to identify.
  • Environmental data can help create awareness about public health.

🔎 Findings & Insights

Key findings from environmental data analysis

6.1 Major Findings

  • Air quality varies across the selected months.
  • PM2.5 levels also show monthly variation.
  • Environmental data can be effectively presented through charts and graphs.

6.2 Key Insights

The analysis shows that environmental conditions change over time. Visualizing AQI and PM2.5 data makes these changes easier to understand and helps create awareness about environmental health.

6.3 Problems / Challenges Identified

  • Different data sources may use different formats.
